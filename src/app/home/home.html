04 / Common questions

Before you order

Which Subaru WRX model years are supported?

Custom tuning is available for 2015–2026 WRX models, covering VA and VB platforms as well as supported FA24-swapped VA builds.

What hardware and software do I need?

You’ll need a free Atlas account and a supported OBDII adapter. The service page links to the current Atlas download and supported-device list.

Is this an off-the-shelf map?

No. Your calibration is prepared around the vehicle information and modifications you provide when ordering.
